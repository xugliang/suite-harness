# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, nationality, appearance, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- discuss ideas and code with respect and specific technical evidence;
- assume good intent while remaining open to correction;
- give and accept constructive feedback;
- protect private information, credentials, customer data, and vulnerability details;
- make space for people with different levels of experience;
- focus on the impact of behavior and repair harm when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, retaliation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Deliberately placing secrets, customer data, or exploit details in public project channels is also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, reviews, project events, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an available private project contact channel. Do not put sensitive personal details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and impartially as possible, limit disclosure to people needed for resolution, and may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a report.

Good-faith reports and participation in an investigation must not result in retaliation.
